## About Me

I'm a Computer Science student who learns by shipping. Most of what you'll find here started as a
practical question — *how would I actually build this?* — and turned into a working application:
mobile apps, web workspaces, games, data analyses and automation pipelines.

I care about writing code that other people can read, documenting projects properly, and improving
each repository through continuous iteration rather than one-off pushes. My background in IT support
taught me to debug patiently and to think about the person on the other side of the software.
